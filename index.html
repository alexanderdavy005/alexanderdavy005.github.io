Exoskeleton Linkage Connector

The Biomimetic Wearable Robotics Lab

  • +94%Connector length
  • 0–45°Ankle range of motion
  • 15 mmMotor relocation specified

What

  • Redesigned the ankle-exoskeleton linkage connector to improve clearance and restore the required ankle range of motion.
  • Addressed interference caused by the original 17.5 mm connector.

How

  • Used hand-calculated clearances and SolidWorks to redesign the linkage connector from 17.5 mm to 34 mm.
  • Specified a 15 mm motor relocation to prevent interference between the linkage and surrounding frame.

Results

  • Increased connector length by 94%, enabling the linkage to move through the required geometry.
  • Enabled 0–45° ankle motion.
